Methodology

Every non-raw metric Freetify calculates, the exact formula, and why it exists.

No black boxes. Freetify does not calculate or claim to reproduce HLTV Rating or Leetify Rating. Formulas below use only locally parsed demo events.

Rating primary metric

(K + 0.5A + Dmg/100 + 0.6OK + 0.4TK + UtilDmg/100 + 0.15EF + 0.8Obj) / R

K kills · A assists · Dmg player damage · OK opening kills · TK trade kills · EF enemies flashed · Obj bomb plants/defuses · R rounds played.

It combines combat, opening and trade value, utility, flashes, and objectives, then normalizes by rounds so shorter and longer matches can be compared. The weights are explicit Freetify choices—not an official rating formula.

Damage Impact

K/D × ADR

Combines frag efficiency with damage per round into one transparent combat-output number. It is deliberately separate from Rating, which includes utility and objectives.

K / D

K / max(D, 1)

Kills divided by deaths. A minimum denominator of one prevents an undefined value in a no-death demo segment.

ADR

Total player damage / R

Damage is normalized per round so it is comparable across match lengths.

Headshot rate

Headshot kills / max(K, 1) × 100

Shows the share of a player’s kills that were headshots; it does not measure all bullets that hit heads.

Accuracy

Weapon-hit events / weapon-fire events × 100

Uses the demo’s fire and hit events. It is blank when no weapon-fire events are available, rather than guessing.

Aim legacy blend

100 × (0.65 × Accuracy/100 + 0.35 × HS rate/100)

A convenience blend of hit accuracy and headshot rate. It is shown for compatibility in the scoreboard only; it is not a validated skill rating.

Utility legacy blend

min(100, 100 × (0.7 × min((UtilDmg/R)/20, 1) + 0.3 × min((EF/R)/1.5, 1)))

A capped mix of utility damage and enemies flashed per round. The caps prevent unusually long utility totals from dominating; it is not a validated skill rating.

Opening / trade differential

Opening kills − opening deaths
Trade kills − trade deaths

Simple net-event differences. They make first-contact and trade participation visible without turning them into a hidden score.

KAST-style value legacy

(K + A + (R − D)) / R × 100

A legacy convenience field based on kills, assists, and survival. It is not a round-accurate KAST implementation and should not be compared to third-party KAST.

Match result and win rate

WIN if own round wins > losses; win rate = wins / (wins + losses) × 100

Match result uses parsed round winners. The Stats page counts analyzed match results, not individual round outcomes, for win rate.

Stats-page aggregation

Rates: totals / all analyzed rounds
Rating and Damage Impact: average per match

K/D, kills/round, deaths/round, ADR, and HS rate aggregate all available player events. Rating and Damage Impact are averaged match values so one long demo does not dominate them.
